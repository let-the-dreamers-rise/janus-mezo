The boost market for Mezo

janus-mezo.vercel.appMezo Buildathon · Wave 2 · November 2026
The problem

Mezo pays veBTC up to 5x more. The votes that unlock it are traded by hand.

No price

Deals happen in DMs and OTC

A veBTC holder who wants a higher boost has to find voters and negotiate one by one.

No guarantee

Pay first, hope the votes arrive

Nothing ties the payment to votes landing on the gauge or the boost being applied.

Idle capital

veMEZO votes earn nothing alone

Small lockers cannot sell their votes, and a lock cannot be used anywhere else.

Janus02
The solution

One market, two faces.

Buyers · veBTC holders

Bid for boost

Set a price per vote in MUSD, a target boost up to 5x and an escrow. The order stands every week until it is cancelled.

Pay only for votes delivered and boost applied on-chain.

Sellers · veMEZO holders

Earn from your votes

Deposit a veMEZO lock, receive jMEZO: a fungible ERC-20 share of every MEZO in the vault.

90% of every payment streams to jMEZO holders in MUSD.
